1.1 miniAuto Introduction ​

1.1.1 Product Introduction

miniAuto is an entry-level AI vision robot car powered by an Atmega328 microcontroller and fully compatible with the Arduino programming platform.

It comes equipped with an AI vision module, ultrasonic sensor, 4-channel line follower, multifunctional expansion board, Bluetooth communication module, and an anti-blocking gripper. miniAuto also offers multiple sensor expansion ports, making it easy for users to explore secondary development and expand its capabilities.

1.1.2 Notes

Instructions on the usage and storage of this product:

(1) This product contains small parts and sharp pins. It is not suitable for children under 12 years old.

(2) Minors should use this product only under adult supervision and guidance.

(3) Avoid swallowing or applying pressure to small or sharp components to prevent injury.

(4) The product includes conductive components. Do not touch them with metal objects while the power is on.

(5) Do not force the robot to move while it is powered on, as this may cause damage.

(6) If the product will not be used for an extended period, please fully charge the battery, then remove it and store it in a cool, dry place.

1.4 Charging Instruction ​

1.4.1 Battery Charging Instructions

For safety reasons, lithium batteries may not be fully charged during transportation. Please charge the battery for approximately 1 hour before first use.

Charging Steps:

(1) Insert the two 18650 batteries into the charger as shown in the figure below. The charging indicator will turn red while charging and green once charging is complete.

Do not reverse the positive and negative terminals of the batteries. If a charger is required, please use one that supports 5V, 1–2A output.

(2) Turn "ON" the battery holder case after placing the batteries.

1.4.2 Battery Safety Guide

(1) Please use the charger provided in the kit to charge the battery.

(2) While charging, the adapter indicator will display red. It will turn green once the battery is fully charged. Be sure to disconnect the power promptly after charging is complete to avoid overcharging.

(3) If the robot will not be used for an extended period, fully charge the battery, switch off the battery case, and store it in a cool, dry place.

(4) Do not modify, weld, or tamper with the battery or charger in any way.

(5) Keep the battery away from high temperatures and liquids to prevent overheating, fire hazards, or performance degradation.

Hiwonder shall not be held responsible for any product damage, economic loss, or safety incidents resulting from failure to follow the safety guidelines outlined in this manual.
